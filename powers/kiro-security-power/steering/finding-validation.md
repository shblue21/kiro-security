# Finding validation
Inspect the finding's scan profile first. For VSIX Fast findings, call `security_validate_finding` and preserve uncertainty as `needs_review`. For model Standard/Diff/Deep scans, complete the assigned model tail and then use the authoritative `validation` and `attackPath` returned by `security_get_finding`; do not call the deterministic validator or overwrite model proof.

For a validation assignment, define at most five decisive criteria and verify the exact candidate instance: attacker-controlled source, closest control, reachable sink or protected operation, security boundary, and concrete impact. Prefer a bounded reproduction through the real interface when feasible and authorized; otherwise perform an exact static trace, name the strongest counterevidence, and record the precise proof gap. Missing dependencies, services, credentials, or deployment evidence lower confidence or require deferral; they do not prove safety. Never claim an unexecuted test, PoC, or observation.

For an attack-path assignment, build the factual chain from actor and entry point through preconditions, identity/privilege, controls, trust-boundary crossing, sink, and impact. Preserve every affected instance and current source location. Challenge reportability with the strongest repository counterevidence, then calibrate likelihood and severity from realistic reach and impact rather than the candidate label or bug class. Keep unknowns explicit and do not invent a longer exploit chain to preserve severity.
